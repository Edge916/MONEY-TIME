# Money Time: first business test

Prepared October 10, 2026. Currency: USD. Status: offer and prototype prepared; customer demand has not yet been tested.

## Recommendation

Test **QuotePage**, a small website service for independent mobile auto detailers. Offer one page for **$249**, with optional **$49/month** care. The page presents three service packages, service area, owner-approved photos when available, and contact or existing booking links. The buyer is an owner who has work to show but lacks a clear, usable place to send prospective customers.

Keep the first **60 days at $0 out of pocket**: use an existing computer, current assistant access, a free Gmail inbox, free host subdomains, and free static hosting. No paid ads, domain purchase, paid AI API, premium builder, or paid stock assets. Processor fees come out of customer payments. A custom domain is optional and bought directly by the customer. Do not commit to paid tools before collected revenue covers them.

This recommendation is an inference from the evidence below. No customers, sales, reviews, or conversion rates have been invented. The fastest useful next step is a paid pilot, followed by a renewal test. Research alone cannot validate willingness to pay.

## Economics and the income target

The planning metric below is **cash contribution after estimated fees and a 5% refund reserve**, before personal income taxes and owner labor. It is not an accounting profit statement or a bank deposit guarantee. Free hosting means modeled hosting expense is zero for the first 60 days. Additional paid services are excluded from the offer. Track actual processor deductions and expenses in the workbook.

US domestic card assumptions: 2.9% plus $0.30 per transaction. Recurring charges also include 0.7% Stripe Billing. Round each fee component to cents. Rates differ by account, country, payment method, and enabled features. Optional invoicing/tax features are not included. Gumroad direct fees include 10% plus $0.50 **and** card processing of 2.9% plus $0.30. Its Discover fee is 30% including processing: a $19 Discover sale contributes $12.35 after a 5% reserve, requiring 17 or 41 monthly sales. At the zero hosting-cost assumption, 5 care customers contribute **$222.45/month**, and 12 contribute **$533.88/month**. A conservative operating-cost test of $15 fixed plus $1/customer lowers these to $202.45 and $506.88. Any future expense must first be covered by sales.

Owner time matters: at $25/hour, 20 minutes of monthly support costs $8.33 per customer in economic terms. Adding 4 hours/month of sales/admin time raises the zero-hosting-cost recurring target to roughly **9 customers for $200** or **17 for $500** after valuing that labor. A build taking 4 hours contributes $129.03 after valuing delivery labor, before prospecting time. These time assumptions need calibration on the first pilot.

The cash target is shared across this business. If each account needs its own separate $200–$500 allocation, the combined target is $400–$1,000. Change the target cells accordingly. Setup income is one time. Do not count a one-time build sale as recurring monthly income.

## Automation and delivery

The included studio generates an original, responsive website draft from a name, service area, contact details, three packages, and a color direction. It previews desktop/mobile layouts and downloads a complete HTML file. It uses browser templates; there are no paid AI API calls. Inputs stay in the browser tab and are not submitted to a server.

The assistant handles design work and revisions in an active chat. The template handles repeated draft generation immediately. Delivery still requires checking the buyer's facts and asset rights, an approved preview, host access, and a verified inquiry route. Publication, customer conversations, and payment setup are not yet unattended. No background agent or recurring paid service has been scheduled.

Use a free Gmail address as the initial inquiry channel. A `mailto:` inquiry link opens the visitor's email app. It does not guarantee delivery or create a server-side lead record. Once the owner supplies the verified inbox address, set `CONTACT_EMAIL` in the prototype and test a real inquiry. A received email is stronger evidence than a copied brief.

Free static hosting is technically feasible: Cloudflare documents 500 builds/month and 100 projects/account on its Free Pages plan. Customer hosting accounts and any domains stay with the customer. The private Sites deployment is an owner review copy; customer access requires a separately authorized public launch or client-host deployment. [Cloudflare Pages limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/platform/limits/)

## 30-day plan and second-month limit

Day 1 is October 11, 2026. Day 30 is November 9. These are planning dates, not scheduled background work. Available owner time is assumed to be about 5 hours/week until specified.

| Period | Work | Measurable result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Owner creates free Gmail inbox and specifies seller identity, market, and processor account. Assistant connects inquiry route and finishes demo checks. | Inquiry reaches the real inbox. Offer and optional care terms are clear. No paid tool activated. |
| Days 4–10 | Research 20 qualifying businesses in one chosen market. Prepare individual observations and use owner-authorized outreach or warm introductions. | 20 qualification records and actual contact outcomes. No invented leads. |
| Days 11–14 | Seek 5 conversations. Present the same $249 offer and $49 optional care. Record reasons for refusal. | 1 paid pilot objective. Apply stop/change rules if there is no buying signal. |
| Days 15–21 | Deliver the first pilot in roughly 4 hours, check owner-approved content and contact path, collect final payment, and record fees/time. | Approved, functioning customer site and actual contribution calculation. |
| Days 22–30 | Ask for a referral after successful delivery. Reuse the design for a second buyer. Offer care only where updates are useful. | Second paid pilot objective, actual care opt-in count, and delivery-time evidence. |
| Days 31–60 | Repeat the same offer, check the first care renewal, measure churn and acquisition time. | Aim toward 5 paid care clients; only actual paid renewals count. Keep new out-of-pocket spending at $0. |

One or two paid pilots would validate a starting offer, not stable $200–$500 monthly income. If care uptake is 40%, five care customers may require about 13 delivered sites. That percentage is a sensitivity assumption, not observed conversion. At two builds/week, 12 care clients could still take several months. Adjust the pace to actual time and retention.

## Tracking and next work

Acceptance: real inquiry test, responsive/keyboard checks, no placeholders in contact links, no payments implied before processor setup. Scopes: `src/website`, `docs/launch`.

Acceptance: complete funnel evidence, objections, and the paid-pilot decision. Scope: `docs/customer-validation`. Depends on the inquiry-ready pilot.
3. **Deliver the first paid customer website**: owner-approved content, reusable page customization, client-owned host, working contact route, payment/time record. Acceptance: customer approval and actual contribution. Scope: `clients/pilot-01`. Depends on a genuine paid-pilot commitment.
4. **Validate recurring care**: deliver the promised update/check, record an opt-in and renewal, measure support minutes and churn. Acceptance: a paid renewal and updated economics. Scope: `docs/care-validation`. Depends on a delivered customer site.
